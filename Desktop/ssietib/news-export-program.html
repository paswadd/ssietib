Export Facilitation Program Sees Early Success

Published 20 Sep 2026

SSIETIB's newly launched Export Facilitation Program is already yielding positive results for local businesses. Designed to cut red tape and provide logistical support, the program has successfully assisted local merchants in completing their first shipments to Asian markets.

By advising on policy, customs compliance, and international standards, the Bureau is significantly reducing the barriers to entry for South Sudanese exporters. The program's early success highlights the immense potential of South Sudan's non-oil export sectors, particularly in forestry, livestock, and raw materials.
