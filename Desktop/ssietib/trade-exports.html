Trade & Exports

Connecting South Sudanese producers and exporters with regional and international buyers, and helping international partners source with confidence.

Start a trade enquiry Explore export sectors

Overview

Growing South Sudan’s trade footprint

SSIETIB works across the trade value chain — from building exporter readiness to opening doors with international buyers. We help South Sudanese enterprises meet market requirements and connect them with credible partners abroad.

For international companies, we provide the local knowledge and introductions needed to source responsibly and reliably from South Sudan.

How we help

Trade promotion services

Buyer Matchmaking

Market access

Routes to regional & global markets

South Sudan is a member of regional economic communities and continental trade frameworks that open access to a wide network of markets. SSIETIB helps exporters understand and use these routes.

Mombasa Corridor

Access to the Indian Ocean via Kenya and Uganda.

Djibouti Corridor

Gateway via Ethiopia to the Red Sea.

Port Sudan Corridor

Northern route serving Red Sea markets.

Regional Blocs

EAC, IGAD and AfCFTA preferences.
