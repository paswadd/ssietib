About SSIETIB

An independent, non-governmental bureau established to advance South Sudan’s economic diplomacy, trade and investment agenda on the world stage.

Who we are

An Independent Bureau Advancing South Sudan’s Economic Diplomacy

The South Sudan International Economic, Trade and Investment Bureau (SSIETIB) is an independent, private, non-governmental and non-profit international economic and business promotion organization dedicated to advancing South Sudan’s economic diplomacy, international trade, responsible investment and commercial cooperation. Established as a credible, neutral and professionally managed platform, the Bureau connects South Sudanese enterprises, investors, exporters, manufacturers, financial institutions, business associations and development partners with counterparties across Africa, Asia, the Middle East, Europe and other international markets.

SSIETIB works through business networking, investment promotion, trade facilitation, market intelligence, economic research and institutional collaboration. We help translate South Sudan’s commercial potential into practical opportunities: identifying credible partners, preparing reliable market information, organising trade missions and investment forums, and facilitating introductions that can grow into durable business agreements.

The Bureau is not a ministry, government department, statutory commission, diplomatic mission, regulatory authority or public agency. It does not issue approvals, licences or guarantees, and it does not represent any government unless expressly authorised in writing by the competent authority. Our role is to facilitate lawful private-sector engagement, provide evidence-based information and promote responsible investment that contributes to inclusive and sustainable development for the people of South Sudan.

Guided by integrity, independence and transparency, SSIETIB maintains a clear separation between business facilitation and official regulatory approval. We engage with public institutions where appropriate and lawful, while preserving our independent institutional status and the trust of every stakeholder we serve.

Our direction

Vision, mission and strategic purpose

Our Vision

To become a credible international platform connecting South Sudan with global markets, investment partners and opportunities for sustainable economic development.

Our Mission

To facilitate trade, investment promotion, business partnerships, economic research and international cooperation involving South Sudan through professional networking, information sharing and responsible business facilitation.

Our Strategic Purpose

To mobilise international economic cooperation around South Sudan’s priorities by connecting opportunity with capability: linking local enterprises to global markets and capital, helping investors understand the country’s risks and realities, and promoting commercial relationships that create jobs, transfer skills and broaden economic participation.

What guides us

Our six core values

These principles shape how we work, how we engage partners and how we measure our contribution.

Integrity

We act honestly, ethically and accountably, and we do not make claims we cannot substantiate.

Independence

We are a private, non-governmental body; our analysis and advice are not directed by any government or political interest.

Transparency

We communicate our status, mandate, methods and limitations clearly, distinguishing confirmed information from proposals.

Cooperation

We work with businesses, associations and institutions to create shared value while respecting the role of every partner.

Inclusiveness

We promote fair access to opportunity for enterprises of all sizes, including women- and youth-led businesses and communities across South Sudan.

Sustainable Development

We encourage responsible investment and commercial practices that protect resources, create lasting livelihoods and support long-term economic resilience.

Our mandate

Our Institutional Mandate

Our mandate is organised around six mutually reinforcing pillars. Each is delivered within the scope of SSIETIB’s governing documents and applicable law, and never in place of official regulatory approval.

Pillar 01

Investment Promotion

We present lawful investment opportunities to credible investors, support project preparation and match capital with capable enterprises. Our guidance covers market entry, sector conditions and the risks that require independent due diligence.

Pillar 02

Trade Development

We help South Sudanese producers and exporters become market-ready, connect with verified buyers and distributors, and take part in trade exhibitions, buyer missions and regional value chains.

Pillar 03

Business Partnerships

We facilitate B2B introductions, joint-venture discussions and institutional alliances, preserving confidentiality and distinguishing verified partners from prospective relationships.

Pillar 04

Economic Diplomacy

We advance South Sudan’s economic interests through non-governmental engagement with chambers, associations, investors and international economic forums, promoting dialogue without exercising official diplomatic functions.

Pillar 05

Private-Sector Development

We support enterprise growth, entrepreneurship and capacity building, with emphasis on local suppliers, small and medium-sized enterprises, and women- and youth-led businesses.

Pillar 06

Research and Intelligence

We produce market briefs, sector studies and investment guides based on verifiable information, giving stakeholders a realistic view of opportunities, constraints and legal requirements.

How we work

Our Approach

SSIETIB approaches every engagement as a facilitator and information provider. We are careful about what we can verify, explicit about what we cannot promise, and disciplined about the boundary between commercial facilitation and public authority.

The following principles guide our work with investors, exporters, local enterprises, associations and institutional partners.

  • Professional integrity in every engagement.
  • Transparency about our status, mandate and limitations.
  • Responsible investment and respect for applicable law.
  • Confidentiality in business introductions and sensitive information.
  • Fair and inclusive commercial engagement.
  • Evidence-based information and independent due diligence.
  • Clear separation between facilitation and official regulatory approval.
  • Accountability to members, partners and the communities we serve.
Our commitment

Our Commitment to South Sudan

South Sudan’s long-term prosperity will be built by its people and its enterprises. SSIETIB is committed to promoting opportunities that create jobs, build local capacity and widen participation in the economy. We will present the country accurately and responsibly, highlight lawful and commercially viable opportunities, and avoid speculation or unsupported claims.

We will measure our contribution by the quality of the connections we make, the reliability of the information we share and the durable partnerships that result. Our work is always undertaken with respect for South Sudan’s laws, its communities and the independence of our institution.

In practice, this means

  • ◆ Promoting verified opportunities, not invented projects.
  • ◆ Building local supplier capacity alongside investor interest.
  • ◆ Protecting the Bureau’s independence and neutral status.
  • ◆ Encouraging responsible, long-term commercial relationships.
Leadership & governance

Governed with independence and accountability

SSIETIB’s work is overseen by a General Assembly and a Governing Board, supported by an Executive Management team and Secretariat, with dedicated leadership for South Sudan and our China Office. Standing and ad hoc committees provide focused oversight of finance, programmes, research and ethics.
