About SSIETIB

An independent, non-governmental bureau established to advance South Sudan’s economic diplomacy, trade and investment agenda on the world stage.

Our story

Building bridges between South Sudan and the global economy

SSIETIB was founded to provide a credible, neutral platform for international economic engagement with South Sudan. We work with investors, exporters, governments, financiers and development institutions to turn interest into tangible commercial outcomes.

Our work spans market intelligence, investor facilitation, trade promotion, policy dialogue and institutional partnerships. We are guided by transparency, independence and a long-term commitment to inclusive growth.

Our Mission

To promote and facilitate international trade, responsible investment and economic cooperation that advance sustainable and inclusive development for the people of South Sudan.

Our Vision

A connected, competitive and prosperous South Sudan recognised globally as a trusted and attractive destination for trade and investment.

What guides us

Our values

Integrity

We act with honesty, ethics and accountability in every engagement.

Independence

We remain neutral and objective, serving the broader public interest.

Collaboration

We build partnerships that create shared and lasting value.

Excellence

We deliver professional, evidence-based and high-quality services.

Our mandate

What we are mandated to do

  • ◆ Promote South Sudan as a trade and investment destination.
  • ◆ Facilitate linkages between local and international enterprises.
  • ◆ Advise on policy and the ease of doing business.
  • ◆ Represent members in international economic forums.
  • ◆ Build capacity within public and private institutions.
