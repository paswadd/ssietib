Minerals & Mining

Minerals & Mining

A large, lightly explored geological base offers potential in gold, diamonds, base metals and industrial minerals for responsible investors.

Sector overview

South Sudan's minerals and mining potential

South Sudan's geology includes ancient greenstone belts and sedimentary basins with reported occurrences of gold, diamonds, iron ore, copper, nickel, limestone and clay. Large parts of the country have seen little modern exploration, so public data is limited and early-stage work is required to define resources and convert them into bankable projects.

Artisanal mining is already active in several states, while industrial minerals such as limestone and clay can serve domestic cement and construction demand. Success depends on transparent licensing, community benefit agreements, reliable power and transport, and environmental safeguards. Investors who combine exploration with local processing and clear social commitments are best placed to build durable operations.

Underexplored geology

Greenstone belts and basins remain widely under-surveyed by modern exploration methods.

Mineral range

Gold, diamonds, base metals and industrial minerals offer a diversified pipeline.

Infrastructure need

Power, roads and handling facilities determine which deposits can be developed economically.

Value chains

Key sub-sectors

Gold & diamonds

Alluvial and hard-rock occurrences with established artisanal activity.

Iron ore & steel inputs

Banded iron formations with potential for future direct-reduction feedstock.

Base metals

Copper, nickel and associated minerals in Proterozoic greenstone belts.

Industrial minerals

Limestone, clay, silica and gypsum for cement, ceramics and construction.

Oilfield services

Drilling, workover, pipelines and field services for established petroleum operations.

Artisanal mining formalisation

Cooperatives, training, equipment and safer practices for local miners.

Investment focus

Priority opportunities

SSIETIB prioritises responsible projects with clear tenure, community consent and a credible route from exploration to processing and market.

  1. 1

    Greenfield exploration programmes using modern geophysical and geochemical datasets.

  2. 2

    Formalised artisanal and small-scale mining with cooperatives and safer processing.

  3. 3

    Cement and building materials based on local limestone and clay deposits.

  4. 4

    Mineral processing and concentrate handling near reliable transport corridors.

  5. 5

    Oilfield services and local-content partnerships with existing operators.

Facilitation

How SSIETIB supports mining investors

We help investors navigate early-stage uncertainty with practical information, credible introductions and clear guidance on licensing and community engagement.

  • Compilation of available geological, licensing and corridor information.

  • Introductions to ministries, regulators, local authorities and affected communities.

  • Guidance on exploration permits, land access, environment and benefit-sharing.

  • Market intelligence on commodity prices, logistics costs and local-content rules.

  • Field visits and stakeholder roundtables coordinated with local stakeholders.
