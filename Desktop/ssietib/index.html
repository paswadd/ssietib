Who we are

An Independent Bureau Advancing South Sudan’s Economic Diplomacy

The South Sudan International Economic, Trade and Investment Bureau (SSIETIB) is an independent, private, non-governmental and non-profit international economic and business promotion organization dedicated to advancing South Sudan’s economic diplomacy, international trade, responsible investment and commercial cooperation. We connect South Sudanese enterprises, investors, exporters, manufacturers, financial institutions, business associations and development partners with counterparties across Africa, Asia, the Middle East, Europe and other markets.

Through business networking, investment promotion, trade facilitation, market intelligence, economic research and institutional collaboration, we turn commercial potential into practical opportunities. We are not a government agency, do not issue approvals or licences, and do not represent any government unless expressly authorised in writing by the competent authority.

Our Vision

To become a credible international platform connecting South Sudan with global markets, investment partners and opportunities for sustainable economic development.

Our Mission

To facilitate trade, investment promotion, business partnerships, economic research and international cooperation involving South Sudan through professional networking, information sharing and responsible business facilitation.

Our Strategic Purpose

To mobilise international economic cooperation around South Sudan’s priorities by connecting opportunity with capability: linking local enterprises to global markets and capital, helping investors understand the country’s risks and realities, and promoting commercial relationships that create jobs, transfer skills and broaden economic participation.

What we do

Our Institutional Mandate

Six mutually reinforcing pillars guide our work, all delivered within our lawful mandate and without replacing official regulatory approval.

Pillar 01

Investment Promotion

We present lawful opportunities to credible investors, support project preparation and match capital with capable enterprises.

Pillar 02

Trade Development

We help producers become market-ready, connect with verified buyers and join trade missions, exhibitions and regional value chains.

Pillar 03

Business Partnerships

We facilitate B2B introductions, joint ventures and institutional alliances while protecting confidentiality.

Pillar 04

Economic Diplomacy

We advance South Sudan’s economic interests through non-governmental engagement with chambers, investors and economic forums.

Pillar 05

Private-Sector Development

We support enterprise growth, entrepreneurship and capacity building, with a focus on local suppliers and inclusive participation.

Pillar 06

Research and Intelligence

We publish market briefs, sector studies and investment guides based on verifiable information and realistic risk assessment.
