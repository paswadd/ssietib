Who we are

An independent bureau advancing South Sudan’s economic diplomacy.

The South Sudan International Economic, Trade and Investment Bureau (SSIETIB) is an independent, private, non-governmental, and non-profit international economic and business promotion organization dedicated to advancing trade, attracting investment, strengthening international partnerships, and supporting private-sector development in South Sudan. Through business networking, investment promotion, trade facilitation, economic cooperation, and strategic partnerships, SSIETIB works as a trusted bridge — providing market intelligence, matchmaking and advocacy that help enterprises and institutions participate confidently in South Sudan’s growth story.

Learn more about our mandate →

Mission

To facilitate trade and investment that drives inclusive, sustainable growth for South Sudan.

Vision

A connected South Sudan recognised as a competitive and reliable economic partner.

Integrity

Transparent, accountable engagement that protects the interests of all stakeholders.

Partnership

Collaboration across borders, sectors and institutions to create shared value.

What we do

Core focus areas

Integrated services that support the full lifecycle of trade and investment engagement.

Investment Facilitation

Project pipelines, investor matchmaking and end-to-end facilitation support.

Trade Promotion

Export readiness, trade missions, exhibitions and buyer–seller matchmaking.

Market Intelligence

Research, briefings and data that de-risk decisions and reveal opportunity.

Economic Cooperation

Bilateral and multilateral engagement, MoUs and institutional partnerships.

Policy Advisory

Advocacy and reform dialogue to improve the ease of doing business.

Capacity Building

Training and technical assistance for enterprises and public institutions.
