International Cooperation

Building bilateral and multilateral economic partnerships that open markets, mobilise investment and strengthen South Sudan’s global connections.

Our approach

Partnerships across borders

SSIETIB facilitates economic cooperation between South Sudan and partner countries, institutions and regional bodies. We support the design and delivery of agreements, delegations and joint programmes that translate diplomatic goodwill into commercial outcomes.

Our work is organised around dedicated country and regional desks, with China a flagship programme.

Pillars

Areas of cooperation

Bilateral Trade Relations

Country-to-country trade dialogue, market access and business councils.

Multilateral Engagement

Participation in regional and continental economic frameworks.

Development Partners

Coordination with donors and development finance institutions.

Investment Partnerships

Joint ventures, co-investment and technology transfer.

Delegations & Forums

Organising high-level visits, forums and business delegations.

Agreements Support

Facilitating MoUs and cooperation frameworks.

Flagship programme

China–South Sudan cooperation

A dedicated programme supporting trade, investment and people-to-people ties between South Sudan and the People’s Republic of China — from infrastructure and energy to agriculture and manufacturing.
